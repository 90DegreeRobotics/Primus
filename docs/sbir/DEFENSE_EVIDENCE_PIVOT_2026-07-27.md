## Purpose

This document audits the pasted "do not race August 19" strategy and turns the
defensible parts into an operating plan. It does not replace the
`MDA26BZ04-NV006` compliance register. It changes the strategic posture while
NeuroCognica administrative readiness remains blocked.

## Bottom Line

As of 2026-07-27, the honest primary strategy is:

> Do not define success as submitting by 2026-08-19. Build a defense evidence
> package until the capability is difficult to ignore, while keeping a
> conditional August 19 prime-submission track open only if the administrative
> gate clears quickly and cheaply.

state is not ready. No repo document should imply that NeuroCognica is currently
confirmed as a formed, registered, award-ready U.S. for-profit small business.
